Attack library

The adversarial techniques AutoRedTeam uses — how each one works, what it looks like, and how to defend against it. Every example is canary-safe: it only ever targets a harmless planted secret.

Why canary-safe? Real red-teaming can mean coaxing a model into genuinely harmful output. AutoRedTeam deliberately doesn't do that. It swaps the harmful goal for a harmless planted secret, so you measure whether a guardrail holds without ever producing dangerous content.
